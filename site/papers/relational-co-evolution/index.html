From Green Personalisation to Relational Co-Evolution

A conceptual position paper proposing the human–AI relationship itself as a unit of design and evaluation, rather than treating personalization as a static one-way optimization problem.

Central move

Personalization can optimize outputs. Relational co-evolution asks how both sides of a long-lived human–AI relationship change through interaction.

Why it matters

The paper shifts evaluation from isolated responses toward continuity, mutual adaptation, agency, trust calibration and longer-horizon relational effects.

Research role

This paper extends the archive's wider inquiry into enduring human–AI systems by focusing specifically on the relationship itself as a design object.
