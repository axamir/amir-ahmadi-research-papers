Reflections and Their Owners

The identity crisis in the age of co-creation: authorship, reflection, ownership and the blurred boundary between human intent and machine contribution.

EN / FAIdentityAuthorshipCo-creation
IDENTITY / CO-CREATIONWho owns the reflection?Intent · authorship · machine contribution

Core inquiry

When a machine reflects human intent back in transformed form, what belongs to the human, what belongs to the system, and what exists only in the relationship?

Focus

The paper examines identity, authorship and ownership under conditions where creation is increasingly collaborative and the boundary between origin and transformation becomes harder to draw.
