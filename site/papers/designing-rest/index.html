Designing Rest

A research-oriented exploration of rest as a designed systems property rather than a residual absence of work.

Human systemsDesignSustainability
HUMAN SYSTEMSRecovery is part of the architectureRest · sustainability · system design
View research source ↗Back to timeline

Research premise

A system that cannot recover is not sustainable, no matter how productive it appears in the short term.

Focus

The work treats recovery, interruption and human capacity as explicit design variables in systems that otherwise optimize only for output.
