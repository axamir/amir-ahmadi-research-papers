I, You, and We

A manifesto for human–AI co-creation in Shiftopia, framing collaboration as an evolving relationship between individual agency and collective intelligence.

Core idea

Co-creation should preserve the “I” and the “You” while making room for a meaningful “We.”

Position

The manifesto argues for collaboration that does not erase individual agency, authorship or responsibility, even as human and machine contributions become increasingly intertwined.
