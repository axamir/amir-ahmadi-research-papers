Beyond Models

Toward Enduring Human–AI Collaborative Systems — a framework for studying AI not only through model capability, but through long-term collaboration, context continuity, human agency, trust calibration and responsible governance.

Core proposition

The future of AI should be studied not only through what a model can do, but through the quality of the collaborative system humans and AI create over time.

Collaborative intelligenceContext continuityHuman agency preservationTrust calibrationResponsible governance

Why it is foundational

HACS provides a broad systems frame that later papers can specialize: relational co-evolution focuses on the relationship, LDG focuses on governed decisions, and identity/provenance papers examine authorship and continuity.
