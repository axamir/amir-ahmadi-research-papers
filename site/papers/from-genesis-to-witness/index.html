Research direction

A meaningful origin becomes durable only when it can be remembered, inspected and witnessed beyond the moment in which it occurred.

Focus

The work connects origin stories to continuity mechanisms, records, evidence and witnessing — moving from narrative significance toward inspectable provenance.
