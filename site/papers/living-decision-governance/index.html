Living Decision Governance

An executable architecture for continuous human–AI decision governance, treating consequential decisions as governed objects that remain observable, challengeable, signed, measurable and stoppable after deployment.

Central thesis

A consequential decision should not be treated as a one-time event. It should remain governable after deployment.

What the framework adds

LDG links AI-agent analysis, accountable human signatures, collective review, stakeholder feedback, measurable thresholds and adaptive authority into one closed feedback loop.

Signed accountabilityWarning / pause / stopStakeholder sensingAdaptive authority

Origin of inquiry

The immediate intellectual trigger was a public observation by Christopher J. Skinner about enterprise AI's difficulty representing human thinking, communication, decision quality and leadership. LDG treats that observation as a starting question, then develops an independent decision-lifecycle architecture.
