## 3. Living Decision Governance — standalone introduction

One of the first research artifacts I wanted the new archive to present in full is **Living Decision Governance (LDG)**.

LDG starts from a simple problem: a consequential decision should not become invisible once it has been approved and deployed.

The framework treats a decision as a continuously governed object — proposed, challenged, verified, signed, executed, observed, re-scored, slowed, restricted, paused, stopped, or expanded as evidence changes.

The paper connects AI analysis, accountable human judgment, stakeholder signals, adaptive authority, and measurable decision lifecycles. The repository also includes an executable Python model, tests, and a machine-readable verification protocol so the framework can be inspected as more than prose.

Paper:
https://axamir.github.io/amir-ahmadi-research-papers/papers/living-decision-governance/

## 6. Launch principles

When introducing the archive publicly:

- lead with the Research Hub rather than the repository;
- link GitHub as provenance/source, not as the primary reading experience;
- distinguish Draft, Working Paper, Published Research and Executable Artifact labels honestly;
- introduce LDG separately when a technical/executive audience is relevant;
