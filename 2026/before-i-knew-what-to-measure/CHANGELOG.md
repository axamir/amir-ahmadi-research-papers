First auditable working-paper release.

### Corrected

- August 18 Mac Screen Time corrected from the preliminary transcription `11h02m` to the source-supported value `11h22m` (682 minutes).
- Space-weather language revised to distinguish solar flares, CMEs, forecasts/watches, and observed geomagnetic conditions.

### Methodological constraints

- No causal biological effect of space weather is claimed.
- Screen Time is treated as a behavioral proxy, not a biomarker.
- Counterexamples are retained rather than excluded.
- Environmental variables are limited to the predefined set: Kp, Dst, IMF Bz, solar-wind speed, and solar-wind density.
- Primary lag windows are fixed at 0, 6, 12, and 24 hours for the next analysis stage.
