# Release Notes — v0.1

**Release date:** 31 August 2026

This is the first audited release of **Before I Knew What to Measure**.

## What changed from the initial working draft

- Corrected the August 18 Mac Screen Time transcription from **11h02m** to **11h22m** (682 minutes), matching the extracted dataset and source screenshot.
- Preserved August 18–19 as a major counterexample to the simple `higher geomagnetic activity → lower functioning` hypothesis.
- Added Figure 1 directly to both English and Persian release manuscripts.
- Added quantitative period comparison: Mac use averaged ~9h29/day on Aug 15–22 versus ~5h27/day on Aug 23–30 (~43% lower), while Productivity & Finance changed much less (~7%).
- Tightened the distinction between solar flares, CMEs, forecasts/watches and observed geomagnetic conditions.
- Explicitly noted that planetary Kp is not a local exposure measurement.
- Added audited SIDC source notes for Aug 18, 19, 25, 27 and 28.
- Added peer-reviewed literature from 2017 and 2026 to establish the research background without treating associative literature as proof of causality.
- Added stronger language on competing physiological explanations and the lack of synchronized physiological measurements.
- Locked the prospective environmental variables to Kp, Dst, IMF Bz, solar-wind speed and solar-wind density, with lag windows of 0, 6, 12 and 24 hours.
- Preserved the central methodological thesis: **Anomaly Detection ≠ Causal Identification.**

## Status

**v0.1 is suitable for public reading as a transparent working research essay, not as evidence of a causal relationship between space weather and the author's symptoms.**

Future releases should prioritize prospective blinded observation, synchronized physiological data where available, and complete observed—not forecast—space-weather time series.
