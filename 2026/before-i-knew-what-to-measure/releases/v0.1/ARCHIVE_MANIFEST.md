## Freeze rule

v0.1 is now treated as a frozen research snapshot. Substantive changes to claims, numbers, methods, evidence interpretation or references should not silently replace this release. They should be recorded in `CHANGELOG.md` and published as a later version such as v0.2.

Permitted non-substantive maintenance includes correcting broken navigation or adding a DOI/archival identifier after deposit, provided the underlying v0.1 content is unchanged and the identifier addition is documented.

## Integrity model

The evidence chain for this project is:

`source capture → evidence manifest → extracted dataset → analysis → figure → manuscript → archival package`

Where SHA-256 digests are available, they are used to bind source or packaged artifacts to the audit trail. A future DOI deposit should preserve the generated package checksum and record the final deposit identifier without rewriting the research result.

## Interpretation boundary

This release documents a behavioral anomaly, a contemporaneous space-weather sequence, and counterevidence against the simplest causal interpretation. It does **not** establish that space weather caused the reported physiological or cognitive state.

The central methodological claim remains:

> **Anomaly Detection ≠ Causal Identification**

and the central role proposed for intuition is hypothesis generation rather than evidentiary confirmation.
