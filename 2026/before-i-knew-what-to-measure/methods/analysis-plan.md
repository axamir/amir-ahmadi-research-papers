# Analysis Plan

## Objective

Test whether the observed late-August behavioral state change shows any reproducible relationship with predefined space-weather variables, while preserving counterexamples and minimizing confirmation bias.

## Primary principle

> The purpose is not to find a correlation. The purpose is to make the initial hypothesis easy to falsify.

## Behavioral variables

Primary variables:

1. Mac total active time
2. Mac Productivity & Finance time
3. iPhone Social + Entertainment fraction
4. Active-hour fragmentation / sustained-work-block structure

Secondary contextual variables:

- Device activity timing
- Battery activity
- Self-reported energy
- Self-reported concentration
- Sleep timing
- Exercise / gym participation
- Subjective muscular weakness

Screen Time is treated as a behavioral proxy, not as a direct measurement of cognition, fatigue, or health.

## Environmental variables

Predefined variables:

- Kp
- Dst
- IMF Bz
- Solar-wind speed
- Solar-wind density

No additional environmental variable should be promoted to a primary hypothesis after inspecting correlations. Any such analysis must be labeled exploratory.

## Temporal windows

Predefined lag windows:

- 0 hours
- 6 hours
- 12 hours
- 24 hours

Post-hoc selection of arbitrary lags should not be used as confirmatory evidence.

## Controls

The analysis must preserve periods that challenge the hypothesis, including geomagnetically active days with relatively strong behavioral functioning.

Known initial counterexample window:

- August 18–19, 2026: geomagnetic activity reached approximately Kp 5 / G1 during parts of the period while Mac activity remained relatively high.

## Four-cell falsification matrix

Each day or predefined time block should be classifiable into one of four broad states:

| Behavioral state | Space weather quieter | Space weather more active |
|---|---|---|
| Relatively normal functioning | Control | Counterexample candidate |
| Reduced functioning | Competing-cause candidate | Association candidate |

If all four cells are well populated, a simple direct relationship becomes less plausible.

## Interpretation rules

- Coincidence is not causality.
- A real environmental event does not establish a real physiological connection.
- Forecast intensity must not be substituted for observed intensity.
- Global Kp must not be treated as a direct local exposure measurement.
- Absence of a simple Kp relationship does not justify moving through unlimited alternative variables until a correlation appears.
- A single retrospective N-of-1 association cannot establish causality.

## Prospective phase

The next phase should collect daily state variables without consulting space-weather data during recording. Environmental data should be joined only after the observation period.

Suggested daily measures:

- Energy
- Concentration
- Sleep timing
- Subjective muscular strength
- Exercise capacity
- Ability to sustain deep work

Any repeated relationship should be tested across multiple independent events before stronger interpretation.

## Medical separation

The research question and the medical question must remain separate. Persistent or severe fatigue, sleepiness, muscular weakness, or reduced exercise capacity should not be attributed to space weather without independent clinical evidence.
