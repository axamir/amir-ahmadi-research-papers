# References and Source Notes

This directory is reserved for verifiable source notes used in the working paper.

## Source priorities

Prefer primary or authoritative sources where available:

- NOAA Space Weather Prediction Center (SWPC)
- Official geomagnetic / solar-wind archives
- Peer-reviewed literature for biological or cognitive claims
- WHO or comparable high-authority reviews for RF/EMF health claims

## Citation rules

- Distinguish forecast conditions from observed conditions.
- Record retrieval date where useful.
- Avoid using secondary summaries when a primary source is available.
- Do not cite an environmental event as evidence of physiological causation.
- Any claim about human biological effects should be supported separately from the evidence that a solar or geomagnetic event occurred.

## Key source questions to document

1. What exactly occurred on or around August 25, 2026?
2. What geomagnetic conditions were actually observed on August 27–30, rather than merely forecast?
3. What comparable or stronger control events occurred earlier in August 2026?
4. What do Kp, Dst, IMF Bz, solar-wind speed and density actually measure?
5. What evidence, if any, supports human physiological effects relevant to fatigue, sleep, HRV, or cognition?
