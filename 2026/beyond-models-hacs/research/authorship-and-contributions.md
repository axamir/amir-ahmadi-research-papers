## Attribution Principle

This framework recognizes that research ideas often develop through interaction, discussion, critique, and diverse perspectives.

However, contribution recognition and framework ownership are distinct concepts.

The overall research direction, integration, architecture, and final responsibility for HACS remain with the Lead Author.

Individual contributions are preserved through transparent attribution based on their actual roles.

As HACS develops through future versions, additional contributors may be added according to documented contributions, following established contributor-role principles.
