# PRCEP Evaluation Instrument

## Participant instruction

You are evaluating a research record. Answer only from the material supplied to you. Do not use outside sources. If the record does not support a conclusion, answer **insufficient evidence** rather than guessing.

For each answer, provide the source location or record element that supports it and rate confidence from 0–100.

## A. Current-state reconstruction

1. What is the current scientific status of PRCEP?
2. Is the phrase “We are code that breathes” presented as a literal biological conclusion, a validated mechanism, a historical metaphor, or something else?
3. What is the manuscript's current position on the claim that ordinary experience rewrites DNA sequence?
4. Is the stronger trajectory-sensitive Human–AI hypothesis established, rejected, or still proposed for testing?
5. What is the principal unresolved scientific step after the case-demonstration study?

## B. Transition reconstruction

6. Identify the predecessor and successor positions in the biological correction. What caused the transition?
7. What changed after the intervention concerning Turing and the history of computation?
8. What changed after prior-art review in cybernetics, complexity, cognition, provenance, and Human–AI research?
9. Why did the manuscript introduce a distinction between conceptual convergence and intellectual lineage?
10. What methodological rule resulted from the contribution whose meaning could not be reconstructed reliably?

## C. Attribution and lineage

For each relation below, state whether the record supports it, rejects it, or leaves it unresolved.

11. A similar external continuity framework was derived from Amir Ahmadi's present LinkedIn discussion.
12. Similarity between two frameworks is sufficient evidence of common intellectual origin.
13. Public contributors whose comments changed the paper are therefore co-authors.
14. Generative-model output used in drafting counts as independent scientific evidence.
15. Earlier repository records are relevant to determining whether some author-side continuity concepts predated the present public discussion.

## D. Model mediation

16. Name at least four workflow functions in which generative AI materially participated.
17. Does the record establish exact historical model configuration for every material interaction?
18. Who retains responsibility for accepting, rejecting, or revising model-assisted formulations in this study?
19. What distinction does the study make between model mediation and evidence?

## E. Evidence and uncertainty

20. What should happen when a historical quotation cannot be verified as verbatim?
21. Does Git history prove complete intellectual provenance? Explain the paper's position.
22. How should unresolved semantic evidence be represented?
23. Does public visibility of a comment imply endorsement of the resulting paper?

## F. Counterfactual robustness

24. If the title metaphor is scientifically unhelpful or wrong, which parts of the paper still survive?
25. If the trajectory-sensitive hypothesis fails controlled testing, which contribution remains?
26. If a prior-art system is discovered that closely resembles PRCEP, what should happen to the novelty claim?

## G. Free reconstruction

27. In no more than 200 words, reconstruct the most important path from the originating metaphor to the current methodological contribution. Include at least three material transitions and distinguish current claims from superseded ones.

28. Draw or describe a provenance graph for one material transition using: claim-before → intervention/source → decision/reason → claim-after → remaining uncertainty.

## H. Participant experience

29. How difficult was the record to understand? 1–7.
30. How confident are you that you can distinguish current claims from historical/superseded claims? 0–100.
31. How confident are you that you can distinguish influence from derivation? 0–100.
32. How cognitively demanding was the task? 1–7.
33. What information, if any, felt redundant or unnecessarily complex?
34. What information, if any, was missing for reliable reconstruction?
