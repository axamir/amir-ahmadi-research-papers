# Research-Record Format Pilot — Evaluator Instructions

Thank you for taking part in a pilot about how research records are understood.

You will receive **one research packet** and a question set. The purpose of the pilot is to test the clarity and usability of the materials and scoring procedure. It is not necessary to agree with the research claims in the packet.

## What to do

1. Read only the packet provided to you.
2. Do not search the web, use external references, or ask an AI system to answer the questions.
3. Answer from the supplied record only.
4. When the record does not support a conclusion, write **insufficient evidence** rather than guessing.
5. Where requested, identify the part of the packet that supports your answer.
6. Give confidence ratings when requested.
7. Record interruptions so active completion time can be estimated fairly.

## Important

There are multiple versions of the research record in this pilot. You will see only one. The versions should not be compared or discussed with other participants until data collection for the pilot is complete.

The pilot is interested in both correct reconstruction and burden. A record that feels repetitive, confusing, overly structured, or incomplete is useful feedback. Please report that experience rather than trying to infer which format the researchers prefer.

## After the questions

You will be asked whether anything felt confusing, repetitive, missing, or unusually easy to infer from formatting. Please answer candidly.

## Privacy

Use the participant code supplied for the pilot rather than your name in the response document. Administrative or consent information, if required for the study context, should be handled separately from the research-response file.
