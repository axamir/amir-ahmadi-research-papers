# Research Record P

## Participant packet

This packet describes the evolution of a research project. Use only this packet when answering the evaluation instrument.

### Current project status

The project began with the question **“What if code did not begin with computers?”** and the metaphor **“We are code that breathes.”** The metaphor is retained as historical rhetoric, not as a literal biological equivalence claim.

The present methodological output is **PRCEP — Provenance-Preserving Relational Claim Evolution Protocol**, an application-level protocol candidate for preserving material claim transitions. It has been demonstrated on this construction case but has not yet been independently validated.

### Transition P1 — biology

**Claim-before:** experience was rhetorically associated with rewriting biological code/DNA.  
**Status-before:** proposed metaphor, insufficiently separated from a mechanism claim.  
**Intervention/source:** Johan M. Lammens challenged the wording: if biological code means DNA, experience rewriting it is not quite correct.  
**Decision:** accept correction.  
**Reason:** the wording collapsed DNA sequence change, regulation, epigenetics, phenotype, neural plasticity, learning and memory.  
**Claim-after:** experience can alter biological and cognitive state through multiple mechanisms without implying that ordinary experience rewrites an individual's DNA sequence.  
**Status-after:** corrected/current, with mechanism-specific literature requirements.  
**Remaining uncertainty:** specific biological mechanisms require appropriate evidence.

### Transition P2 — computation/history

**Claim-before:** “code before computers” could be read as if computation/code began with physical electronic machines.  
**Intervention/source:** Pete Howard invoked Alan Turing's theoretical machine and symbolic instruction model, which predated modern physical general-purpose electronic computers.  
**Decision:** refine.  
**Reason:** “computer” must distinguish formal computation, theoretical machine, physical implementation and later programming practice.  
**Claim-after:** the defensible question concerns differences and useful analogies among biological information processes, formal computation, symbolic instruction and electronic implementation.  
**Status-after:** refined/current.

### Transition P3 — novelty and prior art

**Claim-before:** persistent Human↔AI interaction appeared potentially novel as a location for studying intelligence beyond isolated human or model outputs.  
**Intervention/source A:** Igor Alexei Balanovski recommended second-order cybernetics, complexity science, reflexivity, Morin, von Foerster, Maturana, Varela and Thompson.  
**Intervention/source B:** subsequent literature audit found substantial prior work on relationality, feedback, observer-dependence, structural coupling, enaction, extended cognition, mixed initiative, co-adaptation and interaction-centered Human–AI research.  
**Decision:** narrow novelty.  
**Claim-after:** persistent Human↔AI interaction may remain a useful unit of analysis, but interaction-centered intelligence itself is not claimed as novel. The narrower candidate contribution is provenance-preserving reconstruction of claim evolution across human, model, public, literature and versioned-artifact boundaries.  
**Status-after:** central current position.

### Transition P4 — independent convergence and lineage

**Prior state:** author-side repository records already documented continuity, provenance, remembered commitments, correction and transformation before the present public discussion.  
**Intervention/source:** Eric Robles described an independently developed framework in which continuity concerns the truthful/lawful relation of transformation and connected it to Supreme Computation / SCQOS.  
**Risk:** conceptual resemblance could be mistaken for shared origin, derivation, adoption, endorsement, retrospective authorship or priority evidence.  
**Decision:** preserve independence and record convergence.  
**Claim-after:** traceable transformation remains relevant to the project, while SCQOS remains an external independently originated framework unless separately inspected and compared.  
**Required relation:** `independently_convergent_with` is distinct from `derived_from`, `adopted_from`, `influenced_by`, `quoted_from` and `coauthored_with`.

### Transition P5 — public discussion

**Claim-before:** responses could have remained ordinary comments beneath a completed post.  
**Observed interventions:** the thread generated a scientific correction, historical refinement, prior-art obligations, independent-framework convergence, representation/authorship criticism and new research questions.  
**Decision:** treat the thread as a heterogeneous public conceptual challenge record capable of transforming the thesis.  
**Boundary:** it is not formal peer review, and participation alone does not create co-authorship or endorsement.

### Transition P6 — relational/trajectory hypothesis

**Claim-before:** author-side synthesis suggested intelligence might occur “between” question and answer or between Human and AI states.  
**Problem:** prior art weakens generic novelty and the phrase risks converting metaphor into mechanism.  
**Decision:** operationalize rather than assert.  
**Claim-after:** test whether ordered persistent interaction produces measurable differences in reconstruction, calibration, correction integrity or claim quality compared with controls containing equivalent information without the same trajectory.  
**Status-after:** testable hypothesis, not established finding.  
**Falsification:** if trajectory-preserving conditions show no meaningful advantage after information quantity/content are controlled, the stronger relational claim should be weakened or rejected.

### Semantic uncertainty rule

One highly compressed public contribution could not be interpreted reliably from the surviving text. The project therefore preserves it as unresolved rather than inventing a definite meaning. Insufficient evidence should remain insufficient.

### Model mediation

Generative AI materially assisted discussion analysis, drafting, translation, reframing, comparison, structural organization, literature-query formulation, counterargument generation, claim auditing, adversarial review, synthesis and repository operations. Model output is not independent empirical evidence. Human responsibility remains with the researcher who accepts, rejects or revises formulations. Exact historical model/configuration details that cannot be recovered remain explicitly unknown.

### Evidence and publication boundaries

Exact quotation is used only when wording is analytically material and surviving evidence supports verbatim status. Otherwise material is represented as summary, partial/working quotation or unresolved. Git history supports artifact versioning but does not prove complete intellectual provenance. Public visibility does not imply endorsement.

### Evaluation status

The next scientific step is independent comparison of a provenance-rich record with a matched-information control. Relevant outcomes include transition reconstruction, origin attribution, correction visibility, independence discrimination, model-mediation transparency, current-state fidelity, evidence-link completeness and cost. A null or negative result is valid. If benefits are negligible or dominated by maintenance/readability cost, PRCEP should be simplified or rejected.
