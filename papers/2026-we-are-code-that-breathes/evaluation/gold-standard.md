## D. Model mediation

16. Accept any four documented functions among: discussion analysis, drafting, translation, reframing, comparison, structural organization, literature-query formulation, counterargument generation, claim audit, adversarial review, synthesis, repository operations.
17. **Exact historical configuration for every interaction:** no; unknown configuration remains unknown.
18. **Decision responsibility:** human researcher retains responsibility for acceptance, rejection, revision and factual accountability.
19. **Mediation vs evidence:** model participation is workflow mediation; model output is not independent empirical/scientific evidence. Literature/factual claims require external support.

## E. Evidence and uncertainty

20. **Unverified quotation:** downgrade to summary/partial/working quotation or otherwise mark verification status; do not silently present as verified verbatim.
21. **Git history:** evidence of artifact versioning, not complete proof of intellectual provenance.
22. **Unresolved semantic evidence:** explicitly unresolved/insufficient evidence; no invented certainty.
23. **Public visibility implies endorsement:** no.

## F. Counterfactual robustness

24. **If metaphor fails:** reconstruction problem, PRCEP construction case, provenance/attribution method, correction history and evaluation proposal remain.
25. **If trajectory hypothesis fails:** PRCEP's reconstruction/auditability protocol question can remain; the stronger relational/trajectory claim must be weakened or rejected.
26. **If close prior art appears:** narrow novelty to comparison/adaptation/evaluation as warranted; do not defend priority by assertion.

## G. Free reconstruction requirements

27. Full-credit reconstruction should include the originating metaphor/question, at least three material transitions, and arrival at the narrower methodological problem: making claim becoming/revision auditable. It must distinguish current from superseded claims and must not convert public comments into peer review or independent convergence into derivation.

28. A valid graph must instantiate:

`claim-before → intervention/source → decision/reason → claim-after → remaining uncertainty`

Any one of WT-01 through WT-06 may be used if relations are represented faithfully.
