# Final Adversarial Review — Release Candidate v0.3

## Verdict

**Conditional pass for manuscript freeze.** The paper now has a defensible methodological center. It should not be described as validated until independent evaluation is performed.

## Claims that survive review

1. A claim transition can be represented as a provenance-bearing research object.
2. The present case demonstrates a workflow for preserving corrections, interventions, lineage distinctions, model mediation, uncertainty, and artifact versions.
3. The case provides concrete examples in which public interventions materially changed the manuscript.
4. PRCEP can be stated as a protocol candidate and evaluation target.
5. The paper may hypothesize a trajectory effect, provided it remains falsifiable and unvalidated.

## Claims that remain prohibited

- PRCEP is the first provenance system.
- PRCEP is already validated.
- Human–AI relational cognition was discovered by this project.
- Current LLMs are conscious or self-aware.
- The public discussion constitutes peer review.
- Conceptual similarity proves derivation or common origin.
- Experience ordinarily rewrites DNA sequence.
- Git history proves complete intellectual provenance.
- AI-generated synthesis is independent scientific evidence.

## Remaining release obligations

### A. Citation alignment
Every literature-dependent sentence in the publication version must map to a verified bibliography entry. Placeholder or exploratory references must not survive unnoticed.

### B. Archival quote verification
The main manuscript is safe because it largely paraphrases. The discussion supplement remains a working evidence layer until exact timestamps, permalinks, edited states, and verbatim text are checked where archival claims require them.

### C. Contributor status
Named public contributors are contributors to the documented trajectory, not co-authors or endorsers unless separately agreed.

### D. Model configuration uncertainty
Historical model/configuration details that cannot be reconstructed must remain explicitly unknown.

### E. Validation language
Use “protocol candidate,” “case demonstration,” “proposed evaluation,” and “hypothesis.” Avoid “proven,” “validated,” or equivalent language before external testing.

## Stress test

### If the metaphor is wrong, does the paper survive?
Yes. The paper explicitly treats the title phrase as historical rhetoric under correction.

### If the relational-intelligence hypothesis is false, does the paper survive?
Yes. PRCEP and the reconstruction problem remain meaningful, while the trajectory hypothesis can be rejected.

### If prior art contains a close provenance system?
The contribution narrows further to comparison, adaptation, or empirical evaluation. The manuscript already permits this narrowing.

### If a contributor disputes interpretation?
The evidence policy permits correction of attribution/context while preserving the versioned history of the amendment.

### If some LinkedIn artifacts cannot be recovered?
Exact archival claims must be downgraded to working-record or reconstructed-summary status; they must not be silently promoted to verified quotation.

### If PRCEP fails evaluation?
The paper's construction case remains a documented negative/limited result, and the protocol should be simplified or rejected as stated.

## Freeze recommendation

Freeze the English conceptual manuscript after citation alignment and artifact consistency checks. Do not wait for future PRCEP validation to publish the protocol-construction case, but label the validation boundary prominently.

**Reviewer conclusion:** the manuscript is now strongest precisely where it is willing to lose claims.
