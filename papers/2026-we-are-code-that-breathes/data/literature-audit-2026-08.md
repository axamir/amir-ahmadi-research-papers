## Biology correction
The manuscript must distinguish DNA sequence from gene regulation, epigenetic modification, neural plasticity, learning, and memory. Reviewed literature supports experience-dependent epigenetic regulation and activity-dependent gene expression in the nervous system; it does not support the broad statement that ordinary experience generally rewrites an individual's DNA sequence.
