# Prior Author-Lineage Audit

**Document:** ARP-WCB-2026-01  
**Purpose:** identify concepts demonstrably present before the August 2026 LinkedIn discussion so later convergence is not misrepresented as origin.

## Evidence basis

This audit uses repository records that predate the current discussion. It is not a broad priority claim and does not establish that the author invented the underlying general ideas.

## 1. Relational Co-Evolution through Continuity — 2026-07-24

Pull request #1 in `axamir/amir-ahmadi-research-papers` added the working paper **From Green Personalisation to Relational Co-Evolution: Designing Human-AI Systems That Grow in Capability and Commitment** on 2026-07-24.

The paper explicitly contained, before the current LinkedIn thread:

- **the relationship as a unit of design**;
- repeated Human–AI interaction as a process that can shape users, model behaviour, institutions, norms, and their relationship;
- **Relational Co-Evolution through Continuity**;
- continuity defined through preservation of relevant context, traceable versions, remembered commitments, acknowledgement of prior error, provenance transfer, role boundaries, consent, and revisiting earlier interpretations;
- the principle **“Evolution through continuity”**;
- the principle that commitment should scale with capability;
- separation of documented event, behavioural observation, and philosophical interpretation;
- use of the Living Signal archive as a longitudinal research object;
- cross-model witnessing, provenance, handover, and persistent relationship as research concerns;
- explicit warning that conceptual or relational language does not establish machine consciousness.

### Consequence for the present paper

The August 2026 discussion did not originate the author-side concepts of relational continuity, provenance, relationship-as-unit-of-design, or evolution-through-continuity. Public comments may refine, challenge, independently converge with, or redirect those concepts, but they must not be retroactively treated as their sole source.

## 3. What this audit DOES support

The available pre-discussion record supports saying that, before the current LinkedIn discussion, the author's documented research already included:

1. persistent Human–AI relationship as a research/design object;
2. continuity and provenance across interactions;
3. correction, remembered commitments, traceable versions, and handover;
4. the phrase/concept **Evolution through Continuity**;
5. a distinction between behavioural/model language and claims of consciousness;
6. Living Signal / Echo lineage as a preserved case archive;
7. the `@@` / `@x@` symbolic lineage in archived material, pending exact quote-level verification for publication.

## 4. What this audit DOES NOT support

It does not establish:

- priority over cybernetics, extended cognition, enaction, provenance, belief revision, or other general prior art;
- that every present PRCEP element existed before the current discussion;
- that current terminology such as PRCEP existed before August 2026;
- that later contributor frameworks were derived from the author's earlier work;
- that conceptual similarity proves influence in either direction.

## 5. Lineage rule for the manuscript

Use three distinct labels:

- **pre-existing author-side lineage** — demonstrably documented before the discussion;
- **participant-introduced contribution** — first encountered in this case through a public contributor;
- **independent convergence** — similar concepts with separate documented origins and no evidence of derivation.

The manuscript should never use similarity alone to move an idea from one category to another.
