## Literature gates now closed enough for manuscript freeze

### Human–AI mixed initiative and co-adaptation
Prior work demonstrates mixed initiative, shared control, reciprocal adaptation, changing roles, and co-adaptive user/system models. Generic mutual Human–AI adaptation is not novel.

### AI-assisted science / distributed research workflow
Contemporary work already positions AI agents as participants in hypothesis generation, planning, analysis, interpretation, and iterative refinement.

### AI-mediated authorship and disclosure
Current scientific guidance requires substantive GenAI use to be disclosed, keeps responsibility with human authors, and rejects AI tools as authors/primary sources. PRCEP may add transition-level mediation detail, but disclosure itself is prior art and current practice.

### Dynamical cognition
Dynamic-systems approaches explicitly emphasize temporal process, self-organization, development, and non-static explanation. The paper's trajectory language cannot be framed as discovering process-oriented cognition.
