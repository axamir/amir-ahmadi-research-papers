## Literature gates now closed enough for manuscript freeze

### Human–AI mixed initiative and co-adaptation
Prior work demonstrates mixed initiative, shared control, reciprocal adaptation, changing roles, and co-adaptive user/system models. This removes novelty from generic claims of mutual Human–AI adaptation.

### AI-assisted science / distributed research workflow
Contemporary work already positions AI agents as participants in hypothesis generation, experiment planning, analysis, interpretation, and iterative refinement. This removes novelty from generic claims that AI can materially participate in scientific workflow.

### AI-mediated authorship and disclosure
Current scientific guidance requires substantive GenAI use to be disclosed, keeps responsibility with human authors, and rejects AI tools as authors/primary sources. PRCEP may add transition-level mediation detail, but disclosure itself is prior art and current practice.

### Dynamical cognition
Dynamic-systems approaches explicitly emphasize temporal process, self-organization, development, and non-static explanation. The paper's trajectory language must remain compatible with this literature and cannot be framed as discovering process-oriented cognition.

## Literature gates retained as contextual limitations, not freeze blockers

- Machine metacognition and operational self-monitoring: relevant only if the manuscript later restores claims about model reflexivity or self-monitoring. Such claims are currently outside the central thesis.
- Distributed epistemic agency: relevant background, but the paper no longer requires a novelty claim about distributed agency itself.

These topics should be cited if reintroduced as substantive claims; otherwise they do not block freeze of the current narrowed manuscript.
