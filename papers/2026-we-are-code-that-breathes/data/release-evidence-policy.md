# Release Evidence, Quotation, and Redaction Policy

**Document:** ARP-WCB-2026-01

## 1. Evidence classes for release

Every externally visible contribution record must be labeled as one of:

- **Exact quotation verified against source artifact**
- **Exact quotation captured in working record but source artifact not yet re-opened**
- **Author reconstruction / summary**
- **Model-assisted summary reviewed by author**
- **Unresolved / insufficient evidence**

A summary must never be presented in quotation marks.

## 2. Ordering and timestamps

Use exact timestamps only where the source artifact supplies them. Relative ordering may be recorded when chronology is known but precise time is not. Edited comments should be marked as edited where the surviving artifact shows that state.

Deletion, replacement, or unavailable source material should be represented as an evidential state, not silently omitted.

## 3. Contributor identity

Before final release verify:

- display name as shown in the source record;
- professional title only if relevant and source-supported;
- contribution text or summary;
- order in the discussion;
- whether the contribution was directed to the author or another participant;
- whether later edits materially changed meaning.

Professional-profile descriptions are contextual metadata, not evidence for the truth of a contribution.

## 4. Quotation minimization

The main manuscript should quote only short passages necessary to demonstrate a material transition. Longer public comments belong in the evidence appendix only where republication is appropriate and necessary.

Whenever paraphrase is sufficient, prefer paraphrase with attribution over reproducing large blocks of public text.

## 5. Privacy and irrelevance

Exclude unrelated personal information even if publicly visible. The research record should preserve only data needed to reconstruct the intellectual transition, attribution, or methodological issue.

Where privacy, legal, or ethical reasons require redaction, record a minimal audit marker such as:

`SOURCE_PRESENT_REDACTED — reason category: privacy | legal | irrelevant-personal-data | contributor-request`

Redaction must not be described as absence of evidence.

## 6. Contributor relationship to paper

Inclusion means only that the public intervention affected the documented research trajectory. It does not imply:

- endorsement;
- peer-review status;
- co-authorship;
- approval of the author's later interpretation;
- ownership of the final synthesis.

## 7. Correction pathway

If a contributor identifies an attribution, quotation, context, or identity error, the public record should be corrected through a new version. The correction reason should remain inspectable without preserving unnecessary personal dispute details.

## 8. Final release test

A contribution record may enter the release appendix only if a reviewer can answer:

1. Is this quotation or summary?
2. What source supports it?
3. Is its order/timestamp known or uncertain?
4. What research transition did it materially affect?
5. Is the contributor's relationship to the paper represented accurately?
6. Is any included personal information necessary to the research purpose?
