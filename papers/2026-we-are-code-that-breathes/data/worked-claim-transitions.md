# Worked Claim Transitions — PRCEP Case Layer

**Document:** ARP-WCB-2026-01  
**Status:** working evidence-to-method bridge

This file demonstrates PRCEP on concrete transitions from the case record. It does not treat every public statement as true; it records how interventions changed what the manuscript can responsibly claim.

## WT-01 — Biological code: from rhetorical compression to corrected mechanism boundary

### C0 — originating formulation
A rhetorical line associated experience with the rewriting of biological code/DNA.

**State:** PROPOSED  
**Class:** E4 presented too close to E1  
**Problem:** sequence change, regulation, epigenetics, plasticity, learning and memory were insufficiently distinguished.

### Intervention
**Source:** Johan M. Lammens, Ph.D.  
**Type:** scientific challenge / correction  
**Core challenge:** if “biological code” means DNA, saying that experience rewrites it is not quite correct.

### Decision
**Action:** ACCEPT CORRECTION  
**Reason:** the original wording overcompressed distinct biological mechanisms and risked a false sequence-level claim.

### C1 — corrected claim
Experience can alter biological and cognitive state through multiple mechanisms without implying that ordinary experience rewrites an individual’s DNA sequence.

**State:** CORRECTED / RETAINED WITH LITERATURE REQUIREMENT  
**Uncertainty:** mechanism-specific claims still require biology citations.

### Provenance consequence
The final paper should not silently present C1 as if it had always been the thesis. Johan’s intervention is part of the reason C1 exists in its present form.

## WT-02 — “Code before computers”: historical refinement

### C0
The originating question could be read as contrasting biological organization with “computers” as though computation/code began with physical electronic machines.

**State:** PROPOSED / AMBIGUOUS

### Intervention
**Source:** Pete Howard  
**Type:** historical correction/expansion  
**Core contribution:** Turing’s theoretical machine and symbolic instruction model preceded modern physical general-purpose electronic computers.

### Decision
**Action:** REFINE  
**Reason:** “computer” must be disambiguated into formal computation, theoretical machine, physical implementation, and later programming practice.

### C1
The historically defensible question is not whether code-like formalism existed before computation, but how biological information processes, formal computation, symbolic instruction, and electronic implementation differ—and where analogy among them remains useful.

**State:** REFINED

### Falsification pressure
If “code” cannot be defined with enough discrimination to prevent arbitrary organized systems from qualifying, the originating metaphor loses explanatory value.

## WT-03 — Interaction as novelty: prior art forces narrowing

### C0
Persistent Human↔AI interaction appeared to offer a potentially novel location for studying intelligence beyond isolated human or model outputs.

**State:** PROPOSED HYPOTHESIS

### Intervention A
**Source:** Igor Alexei Balanovski  
**Type:** prior-art challenge  
**Contribution:** second-order cybernetics, complexity science, reflexivity, Morin, von Foerster, Maturana, Varela and Thompson.

### Intervention B
**Source:** literature audit  
**Type:** prior-art verification  
**Effect:** relationality, feedback, observer-dependence, structural coupling, enaction, extended cognition, and contemporary interaction-centered Human–AI research already occupy substantial portions of this territory.

### Decision
**Action:** NARROW NOVELTY CLAIM

### C1
Persistent Human↔AI interaction remains a candidate unit of analysis, but interaction-centered intelligence itself is not claimed as novel.

### C2 — surviving contribution target
The candidate contribution becomes a provenance-preserving method for reconstructing how claims evolve inside persistent Human–GenAI/public research interaction.

**State:** REFINED / CENTRAL

### Provenance consequence
Igor’s criticism is not merely listed as “feedback.” It is causally linked to the abandonment of an overbroad novelty claim.

## WT-04 — Continuity: independent convergence without lineage collapse

### C0
Prior author-side work treated continuity, witnessing, provenance and transformation as recurring research concerns.

### Intervention
**Source:** Eric Robles  
**Type:** independently developed neighboring framework  
**Core formulation:** continuity is not invariance of the thing but the truthful/lawful relation of transition; Eric connected this to Supreme Computation / SCQOS.

### Risk
Conceptual resemblance could be misrepresented as:

- proof of shared origin;
- proof of equivalence;
- retrospective authorship;
- adoption without attribution;
- or priority evidence.

### Decision
**Action:** PRESERVE INDEPENDENCE + RECORD CONVERGENCE

### C1
Traceable transformation is retained as an important research proposition, while SCQOS remains an external independently originated framework unless and until separately inspected and compared.

**State:** RETAINED WITH INDEPENDENCE MARKER

### PRCEP relation
`independently_convergent_with` must remain distinct from `derived_from`, `adopted_from`, and `coauthored_with`.

## WT-05 — Public discussion: from “feedback” to adversarial elicitation

### C0
The LinkedIn responses could have been treated as ordinary comments beneath a completed post.

### Observed transitions
The thread produced:

- a scientific correction;
- a historical refinement;
- prior-art obligations;
- a formalization suggestion;
- independent-framework convergence;
- substrate/boundary clarification;
- representation/authorship criticism;
- new research questions.

### Decision
**Action:** RECLASSIFY THE ROLE OF THE THREAD

### C1
The discussion is treated as heterogeneous public adversarial conceptual elicitation: research input capable of exposing ambiguity and transforming a thesis, but not equivalent to formal peer review.

**State:** RETAINED AS METHODOLOGICAL LABEL, PENDING LITERATURE COMPARISON

### Boundary
No participant is converted into a reviewer, co-author, or endorser merely because their public intervention changed the research trajectory.

## WT-06 — From “intelligence between states” to a testable trajectory question

### C0
Author-side synthesis proposed that intelligence may occur “between” question and answer or in the relation between Human and AI states.

**State:** E3/E4; conceptually attractive but underdefined.

### Pressure
Prior art makes a generic relational-intelligence novelty claim weak. The phrase also risks reifying a metaphor into a mechanism.

### Decision
**Action:** OPERATIONALIZE RATHER THAN ASSERT

### C1
Ask whether ordered persistent interaction produces measurable differences in reconstruction, calibration, correction integrity, or claim quality compared with controls containing equivalent information without the same trajectory.

**State:** TESTABLE HYPOTHESIS

### Planned ablations
- history ablation;
- provenance ablation;
- correction ablation;
- continuity ablation;
- order ablation;
- information-volume control.

### Falsification condition
If trajectory-preserving conditions show no meaningful advantage after information quantity and content are controlled, the strong relational claim should be weakened or rejected.

# What these examples demonstrate

PRCEP is useful only if it can preserve more than a bibliography. A worked transition must expose:

`claim_before → intervention → source → decision → reason → claim_after → uncertainty → version`

The central empirical question is whether retaining this structure improves independent reconstruction and auditability enough to justify the additional complexity.
