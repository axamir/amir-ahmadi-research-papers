## Claims intentionally not delegated to literature

The following are case-derived and require project evidence rather than external scholarly citation:

- which public intervention changed which manuscript claim;
- whether a contributor's framework was introduced independently;
- pre-discussion author-side lineage documented in repositories/archives;
- which model-mediated operations occurred in this research workflow;
- the current PRCEP schema and evaluation proposal;
- the classification of a surviving quote as exact, partial, summary, or unresolved.

## Claims intentionally retained as hypotheses

- trajectory-sensitive benefits of persistent Human–AI interaction;
- whether PRCEP improves reconstruction relative to matched-information controls;
- whether the protocol's benefits justify annotation and privacy costs.

These must not be worded as literature-established facts or case-proven results.

## Publication rule

Before archival release, every in-text literature citation should be checked against the exact bibliographic entry and, where relevant, DOI/official source. This map closes conceptual alignment; it does not substitute for final copy-edit citation placement.
