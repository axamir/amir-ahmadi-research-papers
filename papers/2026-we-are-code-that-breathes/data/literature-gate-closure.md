# Literature Gate Closure — 2026-08

This checkpoint records the literature boundaries used to permit manuscript-freeze work. It does not claim exhaustive review of every adjacent field.

## Human–AI mixed initiative and co-adaptation

Prior literature already studies reciprocal human/system adaptation, shared or changing initiative, role allocation, and user/system models changing over time. Relevant examples include work on human-machine co-adaptive systems, co-adaptive visual analysis, mixed-initiative Hybrid AI, and adaptive Human–AI collaboration.

**Consequence:** the paper cannot claim novelty for persistent interaction, mutual adaptation, dynamic initiative, or Human–AI co-adaptation in general.

## Longitudinal interaction

Recent longitudinal work explicitly studies Human–AI co-adaptation across iterative dialogues, while broader Human–AI teaming reviews call for longitudinal, in-context study of how teaming evolves over time.

**Consequence:** longitudinality itself is not the contribution.

## AI-assisted scientific discovery

Contemporary literature describes AI agents participating in hypothesis generation, experiment design/planning, data analysis, interpretation, and iterative refinement; recent systems extend toward multi-agent scientific discovery.

**Consequence:** AI participation in research workflow is not novel here. The paper's narrower problem is provenance of how claims change through such participation.

## AI disclosure and responsibility

Current scientific guidance reviewed for this audit requires substantive generative-AI use to be disclosed, retains accountability with human authors, and does not treat AI tools as authors or primary sources.

**Consequence:** PRCEP's model-mediation fields should be framed as transition-level operational detail, not as invention of AI disclosure.

## Dynamical cognition

Thelen & Smith and dynamical-cognition literature established process-, time-, development-, and self-organization-centered approaches decades before this case.

**Consequence:** trajectory/process language is prior-art constrained. The paper should not convert it into a new ontology of intelligence.

## Freeze decision

After subtraction of these neighboring claims, the methodological candidate remains:

> Can an explicit transition record—preserving superseded/current claim states, intervention source, decision and reason, model-mediation function, independent convergence, uncertainty, and artifact version—improve independent reconstruction compared with matched substantive information lacking that transition structure?

This question is sufficiently narrow to proceed to manuscript freeze work. Validation remains future work.
