## Provenance test for every manuscript paragraph

Before a paragraph enters `paper.en.md`, answer:

1. **Origin:** who/what introduced the underlying idea?
2. **Evidence:** E0, E1, E2, E3, or E4?
3. **Prior art:** what literature/framework already covers it?
4. **Transformation:** did the public discussion change the formulation?
5. **Boundary:** what does the paragraph explicitly *not* claim?
6. **Falsification pressure:** what observation or argument would weaken it?
7. **Attribution:** does a named contributor need explicit credit?

If these cannot be answered, the paragraph remains outside the main manuscript.

## Current novelty target

The project should **not** claim novelty for recursion, feedback, observer-dependence, structural coupling, embodiment, enaction, autopoiesis, provenance, or relationality in general.

The narrower candidate novelty is:

> **a provenance-preserving, longitudinal research treatment of persistent Human↔generative-AI interaction in which the coupled relation is examined as a candidate unit of epistemic analysis, while corrections, public adversarial input, participant independence, model mediation, and transformation of the thesis remain reconstructable in the artifact itself.**

This target remains provisional until the literature-gap audit is complete.
