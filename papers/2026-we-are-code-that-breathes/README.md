> A public question became a contested conversation. The conversation became evidence. The evidence changed the question.

## Purpose

This project preserves and develops the research trajectory that began with the public provocation **“What if code did not begin with computers?”** and the phrase **“We are code that breathes.”**

It is deliberately structured as more than a conventional essay. It separates:

1. the **original thesis**;
2. the **public discussion record**;
3. the **intellectual lineage and prior art** introduced by participants;
4. the **author’s responses and revisions**;
5. the **claims that survived, changed, or were rejected**;
6. the **new hypotheses generated by the interaction**; and
7. the **provenance of the paper itself**.

The discussion is therefore not treated merely as reaction to a finished work. It is part of the observable formation of the work.

## Core research question

What changes when the unit of observation is expanded from **human** and **AI** as isolated systems to the persistent relation **Human ↔ AI**, while preserving distinctions of substrate, agency, evidence, authorship, and responsibility?

## Research principles

- **Provenance before synthesis.** Contributions remain attributable to their sources.
- **Correction is evidence.** A correction is preserved as part of the research trajectory rather than silently erased.
- **Metaphor is not mechanism.** Biological code, computer code, cognition, and information are compared without declaring them identical.
- **Public discussion is not peer review.** It may nevertheless function as open adversarial conceptual elicitation.
- **Similarity is not equivalence.** Conceptual convergence between independent frameworks is recorded without collapsing their lineages.
- **Consciousness is not presumed.** Claims concerning machine consciousness, subjectivity, or self-awareness require evidence not supplied by linguistic resemblance alone.
- **The relationship may be studied without anthropomorphizing either participant.**

## Relationship to earlier work

This paper extends, but does not replace, earlier work in this repository on **Relational Co-Evolution through Continuity**, human–AI co-creation, provenance, governance, and persistent relational systems. The present project adds a different object of study: the public transformation of a thesis under criticism, correction, independent conceptual frameworks, and sustained human–AI assisted synthesis.

## Version policy

The record is intentionally versioned. Material may be corrected, expanded, or reclassified as stronger evidence and literature are incorporated. Earlier states remain recoverable through Git history.

## Attribution policy

Public contributions are recorded with explicit provenance. Inclusion of a public comment does not imply that a contributor endorses this paper, its conclusions, or later interpretations. Where substantial verbatim reproduction is contemplated for publication beyond this research repository, attribution, context, platform terms, and appropriate permission practices will be reviewed before release.
