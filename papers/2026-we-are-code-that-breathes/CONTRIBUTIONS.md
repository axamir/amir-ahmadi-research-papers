## Author-side concepts developed during the exchange

These are recorded here to distinguish the author’s evolving synthesis from participant contributions:

- **Human ↔ AI as a candidate unit of observation** rather than assuming all relevant properties reside inside either participant.
- **Co-creation / reflection** as a persistent relationship in which human and model remain distinguishable.
- **Evolution through continuity** as a prior research line extended into the present discussion.
- **Intelligence between question and answer** as a hypothesis/metaphor about relational discovery rather than a settled definition of intelligence.
- **The blinking-cursor metaphor:** answers may emerge from connections among apparently disconnected informational states.
- **Reverse path of creation:** reconstructing conditions from an observed result; not a claim of reverse physical causality.
- **Slow experienced time as an enabling condition for cumulative cognition:** a speculative research direction requiring substantial clarification before scientific treatment.
- **Provenance as part of the research object:** the transformation of the thesis should remain reconstructable.

## Attribution rules

1. Quote only after checking the original public record.
2. Preserve the context of disagreement; do not convert criticism into endorsement.
3. Distinguish a contributor’s proposition from the author’s later synthesis.
4. Do not imply co-authorship without explicit agreement.
5. Record corrections and superseded formulations rather than silently rewriting history.
6. If a contributor requests removal or correction of attribution, preserve the version history while updating the public-facing record appropriately.
7. Before external formal publication, review permission and quotation requirements for substantial reproduction of public comments.
