# Figure 1 — Evolutionary Architecture of Intelligence

## Title

Evolutionary Architecture of Intelligence:
From Capability to Human–AI Co-Creation

## Description

This figure represents a conceptual framework describing possible transitions in artificial intelligence evolution.

The architecture moves through seven layers:

1. Capability
   - Information processing and problem solving.

2. Context
   - Connecting information with environment and meaning.

3. Continuity
   - Allowing previous interactions and experiences to influence future states.

4. Agency
   - Enabling goal-oriented action.

5. Autonomy
   - Supporting independent operation.

6. Governance
   - Ensuring responsibility, transparency, and alignment.

7. Co-Creation
   - Enabling collaborative intelligence between humans and artificial systems.

This is not a technological roadmap.
It is a conceptual model for analyzing future intelligence systems.
