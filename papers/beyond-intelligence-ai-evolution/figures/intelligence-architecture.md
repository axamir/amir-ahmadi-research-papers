## Layer Explanation

### Capability
The ability to process information, recognize patterns, generate outputs, and solve problems.

### Context
The ability to interpret information within relationships, situations, and objectives.

### Continuity
The ability to maintain meaningful connections between experiences over time.

### Agency
The ability to pursue objectives and take actions.

### Governance
The structures that ensure accountability, transparency, safety, and alignment.

### Co-Creation
A collaborative environment where human and artificial intelligence contribute complementary strengths.

---

## Conceptual Purpose

This model does not represent a fixed technological roadmap.

It is a framework for discussing how intelligence may evolve from isolated capability toward responsible collaboration.
