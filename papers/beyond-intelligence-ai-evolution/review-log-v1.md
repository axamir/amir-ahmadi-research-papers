## Conceptual Consistency

### Continuity

Defined as:

The ability of experience and information to influence future states through meaningful interaction over time.

### Co-Creation

Defined as:

A collaborative relationship where human and artificial intelligence contribute complementary strengths toward shared outcomes.

### White Intelligence

Positioned as:

A conceptual framework for designing intelligent systems around responsibility, transparency, protection, and human flourishing.

---

## Claim Calibration

The paper avoids:

- deterministic predictions,
- claims of artificial consciousness,
- assumptions that AI must become human-like.

The paper focuses on:

- conceptual frameworks,
- possible architectures,
- future research questions.
