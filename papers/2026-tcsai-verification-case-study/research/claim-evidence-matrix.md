## Update rule

This table is **evidence-responsive**. A status is not a verdict about identity or motive. If a reproducible new measurement, implementation disclosure, controlled prediction, independent replication, or valid narrowing of a claim appears, the corresponding row should be updated and the reason recorded in the claim-migration log.
