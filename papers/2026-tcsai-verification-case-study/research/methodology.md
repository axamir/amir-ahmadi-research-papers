# Methodology

## Scope

This investigation evaluates the behavior and evidential claims exposed through the publicly accessible NeuroSapiens+ / TCSAI interface and the public claims/challenges made around it. It does not claim access to undisclosed source code, private infrastructure, hidden instrumentation, or proprietary backend systems.

## Core evidential chain

`CLAIM → OBSERVATION → MEASUREMENT → CALCULATION → PREDICTION → FALSIFICATION → INDEPENDENT REPRODUCTION`

The investigation asks where this chain can be demonstrated and where it breaks under the tested conditions.

## Rules imposed on the investigation

1. **Unconventional terminology is not treated as evidence of error.**
2. **The TCSAI framework is sometimes accepted on its own premises** in order to test internal consistency without imposing an external scientific ontology.
3. **A wrong answer is not automatically a falsification of TCSAI as a whole.**
4. **A correct answer is not automatically validation of TCSAI's physical or ontological claims.**
5. **Self-description is not independent empirical verification of the described mechanism.**
6. **Reproducible interface behavior establishes reproducibility of the observed behavior, not automatically its cause.**
7. **Unverified does not mean false.**
8. **Observed contradiction or non-response does not establish fraudulent intent.**
9. **Alternative explanations are retained whenever the data do not discriminate among them.**
10. **Any future reproducible evidence capable of changing the classification must be allowed to change it.**

## Test design logic

The sequence intentionally moved from extraordinary claims to progressively simpler controls:

- measurement provenance
- internal-consistency reproduction
- forced numerical reproduction
- exact-token execution
- elementary arithmetic
- deterministic string transformation
- novel in-context logic
- explicit information extraction
- self-classification
- same-session memory
- cross-session memory
- negative-control memory
- falsifiability and competing-hypothesis tests
- implementation-like claims (`score ≥ 2`, `quality_score`, `sector`, `PhosphorylatedKnowledge`)
- cross-browser/session replication

Each new test was introduced because a previous result left an alternative explanation open.

## Evidential classifications

- **Demonstrated** — observed under the stated protocol.
- **Not demonstrated under tested conditions** — the requested capability or evidential chain was not shown; this does not establish absence elsewhere.
- **Observed claim-behavior tension** — a stated claim and an observed result appear in tension, but additional implementation detail could potentially reconcile them.
- **Undetermined** — current evidence cannot distinguish among material alternatives.
- **Requires independent external evidence** — self-report or internal telemetry cannot by itself establish the stronger claim.

## Browser replication

Where possible, deterministic prompts are repeated under different browser and authentication conditions. Current replication includes:

- Safari, unauthenticated
- Chrome, authenticated with a fresh profile/session

Exact textual identity should only be described as byte-for-byte or character-for-character when the raw outputs have been preserved and compared under a declared normalization procedure.

## Evidence preservation

Primary artifacts include:

- original Safari WebArchive
- original screenshots
- continuous screen recording
- browser/session screenshots
- LinkedIn direct-message screenshots
- raw transcript export
- derivative visual/readable PDFs
- SHA-256 manifests

Derivative artifacts must never be represented as raw originals.
