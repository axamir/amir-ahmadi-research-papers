## Purpose and provenance

This is an independent conceptual paper. A public LinkedIn post by Roberto M. prompted the initial question about the division of responsibility among an owner, integrator, and operator in lunar infrastructure. It is acknowledged as inspiration for the question only. Roberto M. has not reviewed, endorsed, co-authored, or otherwise participated in this paper.

The paper's contribution is the proposed fourth layer—**Continuity**—and its associated constructs: continuity governance, epistemic lineage, responsibility lineage, critical-knowledge access, and the bounded candidate mechanism called **Glass Mind**.

## Scholarly status

This paper does not claim that the proposed model is a proven governance standard, a legal doctrine, or a deployment-ready technical architecture. It is a falsifiable design hypothesis for critical infrastructure that must remain intelligible, safe, and governable across organisational, technical, and generational transitions.

Tokenisation, NFTs, DAOs, distributed ledgers, and AI agents are discussed only as optional implementation mechanisms. None is required by the framework or treated as a solution in itself.
