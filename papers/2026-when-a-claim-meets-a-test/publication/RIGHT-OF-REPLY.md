# Right of reply and correction notice

**For:** Rafael Antonio (Tony) Cantero Suárez  
**Subject:** Factual review and correction protocol — public TCSAI interface case-study draft

Dear Tony,

Following the public discussion and the subsequent examination of the publicly accessible NeuroSapiens+/TCSAI interface, I have published a bounded research draft titled *When a Claim Meets a Test*. It documents the claims and responses available to me, the exact test prompts where preserved, the limits of the evidence, and the conclusions the record permits.

This is not an allegation about hidden systems, intent, or any material I could not inspect. It does not conclude that TCSAI as a whole is false. Its claim-level classifications are limited to: Supported, Partially Supported, Not Demonstrated, Contradicted, and Not Testable from Available Evidence.

You are invited to:

1. identify a factual inaccuracy, incomplete quotation, or wrong chronology using the relevant evidence ID;
2. provide a correction, clarification, or response for inclusion as submitted;
3. provide a reproducible measurement chain, raw-data provenance, independent audit, implementation trace, or a testable discriminator for any claim you believe the draft classifies incorrectly; and/or
4. identify any privacy concern concerning a direct-message excerpt or exhibit.

Please distinguish evidence from disagreement. A correction tied to an artifact, quote, date, or reproducible protocol will be assessed and versioned. A substantive response will be preserved with minimal editorial alteration, except for clearly marked redaction required for privacy or law.

**Requested initial response window:** 12 hours from delivery.  
**If no response is received:** the record will state only “No response received by the stated deadline”; it will not infer agreement.

If new reproducible evidence changes a claim’s evidential status, the record will be amended.

Sincerely,  
Amir Ahmadi
