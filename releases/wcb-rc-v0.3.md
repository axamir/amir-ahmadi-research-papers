## Scope

This release candidate publishes the protocol-construction and case-demonstration study **We Are Code That Breathes: A Provenance-Preserving Case Study of Claim Evolution in Persistent Human–Generative-AI Collaboration**.

The release introduces **PRCEP — Provenance-Preserving Relational Claim Evolution Protocol** as a protocol candidate for preserving material research-claim transitions across human, generative-model, public-discussion, literature, and versioned-artifact boundaries.

## Scientific position

RC v0.3 does **not** claim that PRCEP is validated. It does not claim invention of provenance, belief revision, relational cognition, Human–AI co-adaptation, or interaction-centered intelligence.

The narrow contribution is the construction and demonstration of a workflow for preserving why a claim changed, who or what materially affected the transition, what was accepted or rejected, where model mediation occurred, and what the research position is now.

The main unresolved scientific gate is independent PRCEP evaluation against a matched-information control.
