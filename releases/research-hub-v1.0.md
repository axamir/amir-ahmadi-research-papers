# Research Hub v1.0

Release date: 2026-08-12

Research Hub v1.0 is the publication-ready release of Amir Ahmadi Research.

## Publication surfaces

- English is the canonical international research record, with no Persian UI,
  Persian-edition links, bilingual badges, or Persian `hreflang` references.
- Persian is Amir Ahmadi's interpretation layer and links one way to each
  canonical English research record.
- Eleven papers are published as full-text English and Persian reading pages.

## Included

- Responsive desktop, tablet, and mobile reading layouts
- Vazirmatn typography for Persian pages
- Paper cards, research map, contact routes, and curated Research Network
- GitHub source, revision history, research artifacts, and ORCID links
- Canonical metadata, scholarly JSON-LD, Open Graph cards, and sitemap
- Automated build, source validation, and GitHub Pages deployment
